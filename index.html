Clarivate

- - UX / UI - -
Clarivate is a global analytics and information services company. I was drafted into realise a new rebrand as lead designer. And to help steer and engineer a modular built Wordpress site and design system.

The brief involved creation of research, wireframes and shaping a completely new online language and extensive component library in order that their development teams and complex array of internal departments could administer and construct their own online presence.

Aberfeldy

- - UX / UI - -
Augmented Reality experience for the Scottish whiskey company Aberfeldy. The experience was specially made in conjunction with a limited whiskey presentation box. The user would scan a QR code and an entire 3D environment would grow out of the box. I helped UX and UI the interface for the surrounding application and experience.

Fireball Whiskey

- - UX / UI - -
Fireball Whiskey golfing augmented reality experience. The user would tilt their device to direct the golf balls onto a 3D simulated golfing green. The more points the player accumulated, the greater their chances of winning a golfing trip or branded merchandise.

Clarks
